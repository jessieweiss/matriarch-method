Course Companion

This isn't here to hold you accountable. It's here so your working memory doesn't have to carry dates, progress, and half-formed reflections on top of everything else in your life — offload it here instead, and come back to it whenever you need to remember where you are.

Fill it in here as you go, or print it out and keep it on your desk — both work. Nothing below is timed, and there's no wrong pace.

Your answers save automatically in this browser as you type — close the tab and come back later on the same device, and everything will still be here.
Working across devices, or want a permanent copy? Use Save My Companion as a PDF any time — top or bottom of this page — to save a fully-formatted copy of everything you've filled in. Keep it, print it, or reprint a fresh blank copy later.

Bring your Sensory Walkthrough here

If you did the Sensory Walkthrough checklist — the founding-member bonus you got when you purchased — this is where those notes come back into play. You don't need to redo it or bring the whole thing over, item by item. Just pull the highlight forward: for each room, the one thing that's still not working, in a few words. That's what carries into your Sensory Profile and Clutter Profile in Module 1, and into your Design Brief in Module 3.

Didn't do the walkthrough, or don't have it in front of you? Skip this section for now — nothing later depends on it being filled in.
