How openly do you show it?

Your energy type describes your design vocabulary. This short second part describes its volume — whether that energy shows up the same everywhere, or shows up fuller in the spaces that are only yours.

A leaning toward "contained" here can be a natural preference. It can also be self-protective — nervous system regulation, learned caution, something like rejection sensitivity (RSD). This can't tell you which, and it doesn't need to: either way, the pattern is real and worth designing for.

How to use your profile: Your primary energy is below, with your full distribution across all four types. Most people are a blend — your secondary energy shapes and modifies your primary, it doesn't cancel it out.

If some of your answers reflect learned protection more than natural pattern, your underlying energy may look different than what's below, and that's worth staying curious about rather than resolving today. Either way, what follows is a solid signal of what would support you right now, in this season — not a fixed fact about who you'll always be.

The Energy Profile is a design-discovery tool, not a diagnostic, medical, or psychological instrument. It describes design hypotheses and current needs — nothing here is a fixed fact about who you are, and nothing about you needs fixing.
